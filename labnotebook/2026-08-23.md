### Creating the env
My aim is to faithfully recreate the enviornment of the paper, with an addtion of a plus maze for debugging and development purposes.

New pixi tasks created for poltting the envs. This includes a script for handling the plotting, as well as config env files for the mazes and open circle. `env.py` file is the main orchestrator for creating the environments nd making the importable / accessible. For example, when experiments will be run, the environments will be set, and notchanged. So we will merely import the env and "scene" we want to use. These plots get created under `figures/open_arena.png, and... triple_t, triple_t_rot90, and plus_maze` created by calling plot_envs.py script from pixi task.

======
Dashed lines in the environemnt plotting are the four routes, the model's version of the paper's four dashed white lines from start to goals. The two-letter names encode the two choices a run makes: first letter is the turn direction at the first T junction, second letter is the turn direction at the final junction into the goal.

ee: east at T1, then east, outermost east goal
ew: east at T1, then west, inner east goal
ww: west at T1, then west, outermost west goal
we: west at T1, then east, inner west goal

The middle turn is forced, which is why two letters fully specify a route and why there are exactly four. The gray dotted lines are the two perimeter returns, the paper's yellow dashes. And if you saw an s_e style label, that's the plus maze panel, where names are just endpoint pairs, south end to east end.
